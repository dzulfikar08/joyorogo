# JoyoRogo 1.1.1 store release notes

Build state: `apps/native/app.json` `version` 1.1.1 (bumped because App Store approved 1.1.0 and closed its TestFlight train). New
since the 1.1.0 build (commit `ed6cf1f`): the Joyo app icon, splash and mascot art, the scan's live caption while you talk, telling
the food while the photo is analysed, the kitchen-scale reading for everyone with "Termasuk wadah", and the optional Mode simpel.
COROS and Garmin are allowlisted (owner/QA) and stay out of the notes. Do not claim store availability before the build is live.

## App Store "What's New in This Version"

Indonesian:

```
- Tampilan baru: ikon aplikasi, layar pembuka, dan Joyo di seluruh aplikasi digambar ulang lebih tajam.
- Scan makanan: ceritakan makananmu sambil fotonya dianalisis, dengan teks langsung saat kamu bicara.
- Baca angka timbangan dapur dari foto, dan kurangi berat wadahnya.
- Mode simpel untuk hasil scan yang lebih ringkas (bisa dinyalakan di pengaturan).
- Perbaikan kecil dan stabilitas.
```

English:

```
- A new look: the app icon, splash screen, and Joyo across the app are redrawn and sharper.
- Food scan: describe your meal while the photo is analysed, with a live caption as you speak.
- Read a kitchen scale's display from the photo and take off the container's weight.
- Simple mode for a shorter scan result (turn it on in settings).
- Small fixes and stability.
```

Malay:

```
- Rupa baharu: ikon aplikasi, skrin pembuka dan Joyo di seluruh aplikasi dilukis semula dengan lebih tajam.
- Imbasan makanan: ceritakan makanan anda semasa foto dianalisis, dengan kapsyen langsung semasa anda bercakap.
- Baca paparan penimbang dapur daripada foto dan tolak berat bekasnya.
- Mod ringkas untuk hasil imbasan yang lebih padat (hidupkan dalam tetapan).
- Pembaikan kecil dan kestabilan.
```
