# Story sharing

The share sheet behind every "Bagikan ke Story" button (scan result, beranda,
rekap, latihan summary, challenge, sticker). Same layout on web and native: a
card preview, a template switcher (photo, sticker, brand; photo only when the
story has one), "Pakai fotoku", and four targets.

| Target    | Web                                                                                  | Native                                                                                  |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Instagram | `navigator.share({files})`; only on mobile browsers that can share files             | Direct Story composer with a Facebook App ID, otherwise Instagram's share target; hidden if the app is not installed |
| Copy      | `navigator.clipboard.write(ClipboardItem image/png)`, message where unsupported      | `expo-clipboard` `setImageAsync`                                                         |
| Save      | PNG download                                                                         | `expo-media-library` `Asset.create`, behind a pre-prompt with a "Lanjut" button          |
| More      | `navigator.share({files, text})`; only where the browser can share files             | `react-native-share` system sheet with the caption                                       |

Code: `apps/web/src/components/story/share-story-button.tsx`,
`apps/native/components/story/share-story-button.tsx` and `share.ts`. The cards are
templates drawn by the sticker engine: see [sticker-studio.md](sticker-studio.md). The
sheet's template switcher is now canvas (Story, Kotak, Stiker transparan) plus a style
row; "photo" and "sticker" below refer to the full-bleed photo and the transparent
templates. The Instagram glyph is
`packages/api/src/lib/instagram-glyph.ts` (source and terms in the file).

## Instagram on native: with and without the Facebook App ID

`EXPO_PUBLIC_FB_APP_ID` is read by `instagramFbAppId()` in `share.ts`.

**With an ID** (release builds set it): `Share.shareSingle` with
`Social.InstagramStories` opens Instagram's Story composer directly
(`com.instagram.share.ADD_TO_STORY` on Android, `instagram-stories://` on iOS)
and passes the ID as `source_application`. The transparent sticker template goes
in as `stickerImage`, so it lands as a movable overlay on the user's own
camera shot or photo; photo and brand render as the full-bleed
`backgroundImage`. No gallery permission is needed.

**Without an ID** the direct route is not allowed, so the PNG goes through
`Social.Instagram`:

- Android: Instagram's own chooser (Feed or Your Story) with the image. The
  sticker arrives as a flat image, not an overlay.
- iOS: the plugin saves a JPEG to the photo library and opens it in Instagram's
  composer. It needs full photo access, so the app asks first behind the styled
  pre-prompt. If access is refused or limited, or the hand-off fails, the system
  share sheet opens instead. A JPEG has no transparency, so the sticker loses it.

Any failure on either path falls back to the system share sheet; a cancelled
share is not an error.

Detection: Android `Share.isPackageInstalled('com.instagram.android')` (needs the
`<queries>` entry), iOS `Linking.canOpenURL('instagram://')` (needs
`LSApplicationQueriesSchemes`). Both are generated by the `react-native-share`
plugin entry in `app.json`.

## Which sticker a meal card shows

A scanned meal has one cutout per food and one of the whole plate. Cards and the sticker viewer show the plate as the hero (the
plate's name and summed kcal), and do not draw the component cutouts next to it, so a food never appears twice; meals without a
plate sticker keep showing the component cutout. A plate sticker that is still being cut, or failed, is replaced by the
component cutout instead of failing the sheet. Selection rules and storage: [plate-sticker.md](plate-sticker.md).

## Permissions

Save asks for write-only photo access: iOS add-only (`NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`
from the `expo-media-library` plugin entry), Android below 13 `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`.
Android 13+ writes through MediaStore without any permission, so nothing is asked
and no `READ_MEDIA_*` permission is declared (`granularPermissions: []`, and
`READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED` is blocked in `app.json`).

The pre-prompt button reads "Lanjut" / "Continue"; the system dialog only follows
it. When the system already refused, the sheet explains and offers "Buka
Pengaturan".

The sheet is a Modal that covers the toast layer, so a successful Copy or Save
closes it and then shows the toast; failures stay inline with a retry button.

## Kartu Progres (a period of the diet)

Opened from the "Bagikan progres" button under the period tabs on Berat (web and native), so the card covers the tab that is
selected: 7 days, 30 days, 90 days or since the first weigh-in. Story 9:16 and square 1:1, templates
`period.progress.story` and `period.progress.square` (`packages/sticker/src/rogo/templates/period.ts`, spec `PeriodStory` in
`data.ts`, bindings `period.*`).

All numbers come from `packages/api/src/lib/period-progress.ts` (pure, tested in `period-progress.test.ts`), loaded by
`stats.periodProgress` (`lib/period-store.ts`). The Berat screen calls the same `periodWeight` over the weigh-ins it already
has, so the summary, the chart and the card never disagree.

- Weight: the change is the **smoothed trend** (`emaTrendPoints`) from the period's start to its end, never one scale
  reading against another. The start is the trend on the last weigh-in up to 14 days before the period, else the first
  weigh-in inside it (then `startsLateDays` says so). Weekly pace needs 7 days, a projected date needs 14 days and 3 weigh-ins.
- Days logged: finished WIB days with food, out of the finished days of the period. Today counts once it has food and is
  never a missed day.
- Deficit: the sum over **logged, finished, complete-looking** days of (burn - eaten). Burn is the plan's TDEE (calibrated
  when calibration is on) plus that day's counted activity (`lib/exercise-credit`), the same numbers Beranda's Sisa uses; TDEE
  is today's for every past day, which is why the card says "perkiraan". A day under half the target is a partial log and a
  day without a burn estimate is skipped; both are counted in the result and left out of the sums, as are unlogged days.
- Fat equivalent (7.700 kcal per kg): shown only when it does not contradict the trend (never when the trend went up, never
  when it is more than 0.5 kg above the trend's own loss) and never on a Naikin BB plan, which shows a surplus instead.
- Privacy: the absolute weight, its percent and the kg axis are hidden by default ("Sembunyikan berat badan", the existing
  `hideNumbers` pref); only the change in kg shows, because change and percent together reveal the starting weight. The
  deficit has its own switch per share ("Sembunyikan perkiraan defisit", not remembered).
- Charts: the weight trend (axis ends labelled with kg when shown, dates under it) and calories eaten per day against the
  target (axis 0 to the top value, per week beyond 45 days, unlogged days as short bars).

Contact sheet and goldens: `bun run stickers:goldens` in `packages/sticker`; fixtures `period-*` in `rogo/fixtures.ts`.
