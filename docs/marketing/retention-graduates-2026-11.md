# JoyoRogo after the first month: "graduates" (27 Nov 2026)

Owner's observation: people who log long enough learn their usual foods and calories, then stop logging daily and
just eat what they know is low. Daily logging stops mattering for them.

Data (renewal check 27 Nov): of 15 lapsed October buyers, 10 logged food on 7 days or fewer in total and stopped
before they could learn anything; 2 logged 22 and 39 days and match the "graduate" pattern (one still logs on the free
tier). So the first two weeks (activation, paid check-in mails) and the month-two "graduate" are different problems.

Agreed directions for Pro value that does not depend on daily logging (owner, 27 Nov):

1. Spot-check mode: scan only new or unusual food, no streak or "missed 3 days" pressure.
2. A weekly look instead of a daily diary: weight trend plus a few check scans and a short summary of the week.
3. Known-good meals: "makanan yang biasa aman buatmu", picked without thinking (builds on favourites).
4. JoyoFit as the daily habit: the watch syncs by itself, so the app stays useful without manual logging.
