## 3. One-hour shoot (target: 20–30 videos)

Format:
- A 1–3 s reaction hook filmed by the founder, then a plain screen recording of the app.
- Lowercase text on screen and a trending sound added on the phone, no voiceover.
- Shoot rules: 9:16, 1080p, 30 fps. 5–6 s takes, 3 per scene. Start the first frame already mid-emotion. Window light. Keep face and action centred (the platform UI covers the edges). No text or music while filming.

## 5. Cadence and judging

**Cadence:**
- At most 1–2 posts a day per account, 4–6 h apart, never more than 3. Never two variants of one winner back to back.
- Every post gets a unique caption, 2–4 rotated hashtags, one CTA, and ends with a real question.
- Cross-posting one video to TikTok, Reels and Shorts on different days is fine. Never post the same video twice on one platform from our own accounts on the same day.

**View rounds:** R0 under 200, R1 200–500, R2 500–2k, R3 2k–20k, R4 over 20k.
- Under 3k after 7 days: kill.
- Over 10–15k, or 3x the account's 30-day median: make 3–10 variants (new hook or scenario, same mechanism).

**Throttle check:** when several posts are stuck low, engagement above ~1% means throttled (pause 48–72 h, then 1 a day). Below 1% means the first 1.5 s is failing.

**Monday review** (siar `list_account_posts` plus Revklik/UTM for downloads). Benchmark: about 5 downloads per 1k views.
- High views and converting → more variants.
- High views, not converting → fix the CTA or bio.
- Low views but converting → new hooks.
- Neither → kill it and log why.
- Downloads but no payments → the problem is onboarding or the paywall, not content.

**Judging:** a pattern needs 6–30 posts behind it, not one or two.

- Each post gets one CTA: the bio link or a pinned comment.
